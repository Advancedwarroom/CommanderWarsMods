name=Cheat Menu
description=Adds a "Cheat..." entry to the in-game menu, directly above End Turn. Eight modules: change weather; toggle fog of war; replace terrain on any tile; unit tools (adjust HP/fuel/ammo with sign and x10 toggle buttons, transform unit type, spawn a unit for any player with chosen HP, delete unit, change unit owner, toggle has-moved state); place buildings and special weapons (cannons etc.) on the map and adjust weapon HP; adjust any player's funds with sign and x10/x100 toggle buttons; swap COs in any slot, fill or empty CO power; declare a player victorious or defeated. Unit, terrain, building and CO lists are queried live from the engine's sprite managers, so content from other mods appears automatically; built-in base-game lists serve as fallback. Values are clamped to valid ranges, HP at or below 0 destroys the unit. Single-player use only; every command is a real game action.
