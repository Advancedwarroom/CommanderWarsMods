name=MoreUnits
description=Adds more units, weapons and buildings to CommanderWars. Includes The Great Owl (巨枭) - IDS ultimate strategic bomber with high-altitude invulnerability, area bombing, and global air raid support. Also includes The Black Onyx (黑玛瑙卫星) - orbital battle satellite with forcefield projection and invulnerability to conventional attacks. Also includes The Grand Bolt (巨雷) - Von Bolt's ultimate Oozium weapon, a massive 19x11 multi-tile structure with 3 weak points, crawl movement, unit production, and supply capabilities. Also includes Ghost Cannon (four facings, attackable only by air and anti-air units) and the Black Arc (blink-animated fortress with 100 HP that bombards any map tile (5 HP to enemy units within range 3; allied units are unaffected); it can be attacked and destroyed, but only by air-capable weapons, and its armor reduces incoming damage by 25%). Also includes the Shotgun Tank (霰弹坦克) - a 15000G direct-fire vehicle whose blast damages the two tiles flanking its target with full attack calculations; splash hits units and HP-buildings regardless of allegiance, splash victims never counterattack, shows only the main battle animation, and costs a single shell per attack. Also includes the Laser Tank - a 15000G direct-fire vehicle whose piercing beam damages two extra tiles straight along the firing direction with full attack calculations; the beam penetrates all obstacles, hits units and HP-buildings regardless of allegiance, beam victims never counterattack, and a red laser ray visual accompanies each shot. Also adds The Black Leviathan - a 20000G Black Hole super weapon that creeps one tile per turn on sea, lake, beach and harbour tiles (MOVE_SHIP; mod movement tables are not loaded by the engine, rivers unavailable). Its Devour action (red field selection at range 1-2) instantly kills a single enemy unit; the Leviathan stays in place, counts as moved, and can devour once per turn, six times in total. Also adds the Matrix Car - a 16000G Black Hole support vehicle with Neo Tank mobility that cannot attack. Its EMP action locks down an adjacent enemy unit (range 1, zero damage, Von Bolt-style hasMoved lockdown): the victim misses exactly one full turn. One EMP per turn, seven charges in total; friendly units are immune. Also adds the Microwave Vehicle - a 14000G allied decisive-battle support unit with 5 movement. In sunny weather its beam uses the heavy-tank attack table and its defence is light-tank class; in all other weather it attacks with light-tank power and has paper recon armour. Sunny-only Energize action restores 1 HP to a damaged allied unit at range 1-2, once per turn. Also adds the Armored Truck - a 12000G allied bank on wheels with no weapons. Its Armor action arms a friendly unit at range 1-2 (never itself, once per turn, counts as moved): whenever the armored unit loses HP in battle, 75% of the lost HP's value is paid into your funds. The truck earns nothing unless two trucks armor each other.
