Includes The Great Owl (巨枭) - IDS ultimate strategic bomber with high-altitude invulnerability, area bombing, and global air raid support. Also includes The Black Onyx (黑玛瑙卫星) - orbital battle satellite with forcefield projection and invulnerability to conventional attacks. Also includes The Grand Bolt (巨雷) - Von Bolt's ultimate Oozium weapon, a massive 19x11 multi-tile structure with 3 weak points, crawl movement, unit production, and supply capabilities. Also includes Ghost Cannon (four facings, attackable only by air and anti-air units) and the Black Arc (blink-animated fortress with 100 HP that bombards any map tile (5 HP to enemy units within range 3; allied units are unaffected); it can be attacked and destroyed, but only by air-capable weapons, and its armor reduces incoming damage by 25%). Also includes the Shotgun Tank (霰弹坦克) - a 15000G direct-fire vehicle whose blast damages the two tiles flanking its target with full attack calculations; splash hits units and HP-buildings regardless of allegiance, splash victims never counterattack, shows only the main battle animation, and costs a single shell per attack. Also includes the Laser Tank - a 15000G direct-fire vehicle whose piercing beam damages two extra tiles straight along the firing direction with full attack calculations; the beam penetrates all obstacles, hits units and HP-buildings regardless of allegiance, beam victims never counterattack, and a red laser ray visual accompanies each shot. Also adds The Black Leviathan - a 20000G Black Hole super weapon that creeps one tile per turn on sea, river and beach tiles (MOVE_LEVIATHAN). Its Devour action (red field selection at range 1-2) instantly kills a single enemy unit; the Leviathan stays in place, counts as moved, and can devour once per turn, six times in total.
